The network desktops ship with a fine selection of free games&nbsp;:
puzzles, reflexes, strategy, building. With no ads or in-app purchases,
they come automatically with each person's [profile](/en/doc/user-guide/understand/profiles/).

:::note[Indicative ages]
These games have no [PEGI](https://pegi.info/fr) rating: the ages
are only guides, to adjust according to the child.
:::

## Playing with others

- **On the same computer**: SuperTux, Granatier, GNOME Nibbles, GNOME Chess, Iagno.
- **On the local network**: SuperTuxKart, ready to use on every desktop.

Luanti, OpenTTD and Mindustry can also play over the network, once the
administrator has opened the firewall.

## FAQ

{/* t:p 713ad3e4036021cce82c7b35b20e731aaa273c52b4c4c385b28e338057359169 */}
### A game missing, or one too many?

The administrator adds or removes games for each account, via
[user profiles](/en/doc/admin-guide/understand/user-profiles/).

{/* t:p 51dfe76c4b48dc20249449b6e2b10bf81eca0682601d77e42f2813c2e061bc34 */}
### Games not installed by default?

Veloren (open-world 3D RPG), NetHack, Dungeon Crawl, chess-tui and
solitaire-tui can be enabled on demand ([`games` module](/en/ref/modules/home/#-darkonehomegames)).
