Access your photos, files and services from a smartphone, tablet or personal
computer. At home, it's automatic; elsewhere, a secure little app bridges the
gap.

{/* t:p 59b3e1130b289a1414c557e33b2652e64bd4b5a39c345bc036b00ef6f255fb92 */}
## Inside a zone

At home (or anywhere with a gateway), there's virtually nothing to do:

<Steps>

1. **Connect the device** to the WiFi or the zone's internal network.
2. **You're done.** Addressing, Internet access (filtered, ad-free) and services
   are configured automatically.
3. **Open the zone's homepage** in your browser: all services are listed there.

</Steps>

{/* t:p 240e99f33bc41575acd8c0b880918ff04b938ee7a5da4d1e4c8d746ce3015c48 */}
## A single identity

A **single username** unlocks all services (SSO). Log in once and find your
access everywhere.

- **Forgot your password?** Ask your administrator to reset it.
- **Mobile apps** (photos, files, email): enter the service address provided by
  the administrator, then your usual username.

## From outside

Outside a zone, the [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/download) app connects your device to the network, as if you were at home.

:::note[Often unnecessary]
Most services published online are already accessible **without a VPN**, directly from your browser. This step is only needed for services internal to a zone.
:::

<Steps>

1. **Install Tailscale** on the device (iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux).
2. **Choose a custom server** in the account settings of the app, then enter the address given by your administrator (like `https://headscale.domain.tld`).
3. **Sign in with your usual identifier**: the network's sign-in page opens, just like for the other services.
4. **You're all set.** Your zones' services answer at their usual address.

</Steps>

:::tip[The sign-in page does not show up?]
Disable "Use Tailscale DNS" in the app while you sign in, then turn it back on.
:::

:::note[Every six months]
For security, your device is disconnected after six months: simply sign back in with your identifier.
:::

:::tip[Administrator side]
Opening the access is described in the [administrator guide](/en/doc/admin-guide/operate/vpn/#administration).
:::
